# PRODUCT

## What this is

Personal portfolio and writing site for David Dew Mallick, a software engineer based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. It has three surfaces:

1. Public portfolio (home, blog list, blog post). The main job: convince a hiring manager or engineering lead, in under two minutes, that David ships real software. Credibility over decoration.
2. Blog. Long-form technical writing, some generated by an internal AI workflow, all reviewed before publishing. The job: show how David thinks and writes.
3. Admin. A private tool for writing, editing, publishing, and indexing posts. The job: fast, clear, low-drama content operations.

## Audience

- Hiring managers and engineering leads scanning on desktop and phone.
- Recruiters who will look for role, dates, location, contact, and a CV.
- Peers who arrive from GitHub, LinkedIn, or a shared article link.

They are evaluating competence, not entertainment. They have seen hundreds of AI-generated portfolios with gradient heroes, floating cards, and invented metrics. The site must read as a working engineer's own site.

## Modes (per surface)

- Home: Experience. The work and the record lead. The interface stays quiet.
- Blog: Read. Structure for comprehension, comfortable measure, calm typography.
- Admin and auth: Operate. Scanability, clear states, zero decoration.

## Content truth

- All resume facts live in `src/data/resume.ts`. Numbers there are real. Never invent metrics, testimonials, or logos.
- Blog article HTML lives in the database. The prose contract (`.article-content`) is global and must keep working: `.highlight-box`, `.pro-tip`, `.table-wrapper`, `.comparison-table`, `.code-snippet`.
- Existing routes must not change: `/`, `/blog`, `/blog/[slug]`, `/admin`, `/auth/signin`.

## Constraints

- Next.js App Router, Tailwind v4, class-based dark mode via next-themes.
- No em dash (U+2014) or en dash as a separator (U+2013) anywhere in the site, enforced by `npm run check:dashes`.
- Accessibility floor: keyboard reachable, visible focus, AA contrast body text, reduced motion respected.
- No decorative motion, no custom cursors, no gradients, no glassmorphism.

## Success signals

- A visitor can state David's role, current company, stack, and how to reach him after one screen.
- Nothing on the site would appear in an "AI portfolio" pattern list.
- Every page loads fast, works in both themes, and holds up at 375px.
